## Main finding
The index stands at 66, but the shift inside it matters more than the number: for the first time since early October the Ukraine war has a working negotiation track with a date. On 21 October Witkoff takes to St Petersburg a package agreed with Kyiv and European leaders, including a proposed three-way summit and the idea of a partial ceasefire on energy. It is a process, not a result: the Kremlin rules out territorial concessions, and the night of 20 October brought new strikes on Ukraine's grid. In the south nothing improved: the Houthis carried out the deadliest strike of this phase, and Egypt's parliament put off its decision to 26 October.

## Hypotheses (labelled as such)
- Hypothesis (mine): a US-Russia deal at Ukraine's expense. Mixed today. Against it: the Oslo package was drafted with Kyiv and Europe, and Zelensky says US missile warnings are flowing again. For it: Trump's "Russia has been fine", Kallas's charge, and Patriot deliveries Kyiv says are delayed. The channel moved from "over Kyiv's head" to "with Kyiv" — new, not yet tested.
- Hypothesis (mine): declaratory ceasefires erode trust. If a partial energy ceasefire is agreed on 21 October, it will be the second such attempt in a month; the first collapsed within four days.
- Yemen: the Houthis hit a rear-area security camp, not the front line. With Egypt delaying and a US envoy, Tom Barrack, saying the US will not send troops (AP, 20 Oct), Saudi Arabia still has no ground help.
