## 1. Main finding
The index stays at 69 with no change in the exact value. The Gulf cycle is speeding up: Iran hits tankers and Bahrain, the US answers on Iran's coast, and three carriers are now in the region. Neither side has yet moved to a new class of target, and the oil market prices the surplus rather than the strikes. The Crimean Bridge strike closed the bridge for about 17 hours but did not put it out of action.
