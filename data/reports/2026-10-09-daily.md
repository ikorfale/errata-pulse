## 1. Main finding
The index rises to 63 on two changes of state. The first is energy. Last week's support, that bypass routes kept Gulf exports near pre-war levels, has gone: Kpler shows the region exporting 10% less than before the war, and Iran's Guards are extending their threat from the strait to the whole region while incidents occur near the bypass outlets. The second is the home front. Kyiv's mayor speaks of "survival", and drones have knocked out parts of Russia's civilian cloud infrastructure two days running (Ukraine has not publicly claimed the Kaluga strike). On the other side, the US and Russia are talking directly and have already eased one sanction (diesel). That is bargaining, not a truce: strikes on both sides' energy continue.

## 3. Links and hypotheses
- **The bypass is under pressure.** The energy score has rested on Gulf oil leaving by pipelines to the Red Sea and the Gulf of Oman. Iran's Guards now openly declare any route they have not authorised a target, and fires off Fujairah and the UAE coincide with that statement. The Fujairah fire is not attributed. H2 (deliberate escalation): + — the stated aim is to make ships follow Iran's rules. That is the logic of pressure, not chance.
- **Diesel as a peace currency.** On one day came a US–Russia diesel deal, a report of Russia's price for an energy truce, and continuing strikes on both sides' energy. Energy is both the battlefield and the bargaining chip. H4 (overlapping interests), weak +: the deal suits both administrations (US fuel prices before the midterms, revenue for Russia). This is an observation about motives, not evidence of collusion. H3 (using the crisis), +: the timing again tracks the 3 Nov election.
- **A digital front.** Two data-centre strikes in two days are no longer a single case (c33). Russia struck Ukrainian internet providers and data centres in Kyiv in late September ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-drones-strike-another-yandex-data-center-in-russia/)). H5 (loss of control), +: new classes of targets appear faster than rules for them; none of the truces under discussion covers them.
- **H6 (market):** Brent barely moved although the region's physical exports fell 10%. The market prices in stock releases and Russian diesel. The gap between physical flows and price is worth watching (c26).
