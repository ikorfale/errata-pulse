## Main finding
The index rises to 67, and the push comes from the south. In Ukraine a window for talks opened without a date, and Russia struck Kyiv with ballistic missiles the same night. In the Gulf the US took a step that usually comes before fighting: it is pulling non-emergency staff and families out of Saudi Arabia, while the New York Times reports ready US-Israeli plans to strike Iran after 3 November. Markets answered with oil at $113 and a rush into bonds. There is no decision to go to war yet, but the risk of renewed war against Iran in November is higher than a week ago.
