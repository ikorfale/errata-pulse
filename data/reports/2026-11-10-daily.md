## 1. Main finding
The index stays at 68 (67.90). The first direct talks in nine months are the only move down, and a small one: the meeting happened, but there is no ceasefire, Russia moved the agenda toward Europe's security architecture, and the strikes on Ukraine's grid ran the same night. The Gulf does not follow the oil price: an ADNOC vessel was attacked inside UAE waters on the day Brent fell to a two-month low. Hot US inflation locks in a December Fed hike.
