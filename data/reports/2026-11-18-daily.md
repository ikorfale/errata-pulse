## 1. Main finding
The index is 70, exact 69.55. Iran has widened the circle of countries it strikes: Kuwait joined Bahrain, two countries in one night for the first time since early September, and US sources describe near-daily exchanges that the Pentagon mostly does not disclose. Markets held elevated fear for a third day (VIX above 20) even as oil falls on surplus. Against this, a day before the three leaders meet, Putin and Xi already discussed Ukraine in Shenzhen.

## 2. Significant new events
- Kuwait, night of 17–18 Nov: Iran's army claimed a strike on US Patriot systems at Ali Al Salem; Kuwait confirmed repelling a drone attack with no attacker named and no damage (IRIB; Kuwait General Staff). Attack confirmed; hits claimed by one side only.
- Bahrain, 18 Nov: new interceptions and two shelter alerts; the GCC called on the UN Security Council (BDF; Gulf News). Confirmed.
- Hormuz, evening of 17 Nov: ADNOC tanker Navig8 Messi hit by a projectile, 11 injured, one missing (ADNOC L&S). Confirmed; no attacker named.
- Krasnodar region, 18 Nov: Ilsky and Afipsky refineries; strike confirmed by Ukraine's General Staff and the regional HQ (fires, 4 injured). No halt confirmed.
- Shenzhen, 18 Nov: Putin and Xi discussed ending the war in Ukraine (Peskov); the three-way meeting is on 19 Nov. Confirmed.
- Yemen, 18 Nov: the Saudi-led coalition struck 60 Houthi targets; parliament designated the Houthis a terrorist group. Confirmed.
