## 1. Main finding

The day changed the shape of the risk, not its level. The energy node that looked most dangerous yesterday, the East-West pipeline that carries Saudi crude around Hormuz, is running near full load, and the market sees it ([Argus, 6 Oct](https://www.argusmedia.com/de/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2887089-saudi-east-west-pipeline-at-5.8mn-b-d-energy-minister)). The war, though, widened in geography and in the number of states: ships are being sunk in the economic zones of two NATO members, and Turkey and Pakistan have committed to a military presence in Saudi Arabia. Neither is a direct clash of major powers, but both shorten the path to one.

## 2. Significant new events

- **Black Sea, Bulgaria's EEZ, 6 Oct ~03:00.** Air and sea drones hit two merchant ships about 70 miles off Byala: the Togo-flagged Alfa Watan sank, its crew missing; the Palau-flagged Able (wheat) caught fire, 18 crew rescued, two badly hurt ([AP, 6 Oct](https://apnews.com/article/bulgaria-prime-minister-ships-drones-economic-zone-59f61af472f0379ebe7b1eb39b768c45); [Reuters via Straits Times, 6 Oct](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/europe/crew-missing-after-drone-sinks-ship-in-bulgarias-black-sea)). Origin not officially established; a maritime security source told Reuters it was probably Russian; Zelensky blames Russia. Prime Minister Radev: the war "will continue to expand its geographical scope". NATO declined comment. On 5 Oct a Turkish grain ship burned and sank in Romania's EEZ after a drone strike, 2 dead ([Sofia Globe, 5 Oct](https://sofiaglobe.com/2026/10/05/two-dead-turkish-ship-sinks-in-romanias-eez-reportedly-after-drone-strike/)).
- **Mecca pact activated (5 Oct, Riyadh).** Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan agreed "to move immediately to the practical implementation of the collective defence commitments" and ensure the "rapid deployment" of forces in the kingdom ([Reuters via Khaleej Times, 6 Oct](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/gulf/pakistan-turkey-saudi-arabia-deployment-forces-makkah-pact); [Middle East Eye, 5 Oct](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/saudi-arabia-turkey-and-pakistan-activate-mecca-pact-amid-war-houthis)). No numbers, roles or timetable; Turkey's parliament has not ratified the pact ([Foreign Policy, 6 Oct](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/10/06/saudi-arabia-turkey-pakistan-mutual-defense-pact-houthi-yemen-attacks/)).
- **Yemen.** Government forces, with 100 coalition jets, claim Dhubab, Bab and Hadeid near Bab el-Mandeb (anonymous officials via AP, [Euronews, 6 Oct](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/06/yemen-government-forces-advance-on-bab-el-mandeb-in-offensive-against-houthis)); the Houthis claim strikes on Riyadh airport and the Rabigh refinery; Saudi Arabia confirms attacks on Jizan and Najran airports, 3 injured.

## 3. Links and hypotheses

- **The Black Sea as a second "export war" at sea.** Ukraine strikes Russian shadow-fleet tankers and fuel logistics; ships carrying Ukrainian grain burn in Romanian and Bulgarian waters. It mirrors Hormuz: each side hits the other's exports. Evidence for deliberate escalation (H2) if attribution to Russia is confirmed; for now it is Ukraine's claim and an anonymous source.
- **The Mecca pact is a response to Houthi strikes**, turning the Saudi–Houthi war into a coalition war. The causal chain is traceable in statements; it does not support a "common architecture" (H1). It looks reactive.
- **Loss of control (H5):** neither Bulgaria nor NATO names a culprit; Germany took almost a month to attribute the 4 Aug Leipzig drone (AP). Slow attribution is where incidents pile up without response.
- **Markets (H6):** the restored bypass pushed Brent under $100: prices follow physical flows, not attack counts.
