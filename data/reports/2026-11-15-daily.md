## 1. Main finding
The index stays at 69; the exact value rose by a quarter point (68.50 → 68.75), all from energy. Two things are now standing states: Iran is again attacking countries that host US forces (Bahrain twice in three days, and Bahrain itself calls it a resumption of aggression), and the main gas facility of Iraqi Kurdistan has been down for a week with no repair date. The restraining side is words: Trump may not strike if there are no provocations, but provocations continue. That puts Washington in front of a choice it set for itself at the next Iranian attack.

## 3. Links between events and hypotheses
- Hypothesis H2 (deliberate escalation) +: Iran answers the blockade by striking host countries while Washington says "status quo"; the aim appears to be raising the cost of the blockade for the Gulf.
- Hypothesis H3 (using the crisis) =: the US keeps the blockade and Iron Gate and does not want a new campaign (Reuters, 14 Nov); blockade without war stays a comfortable position while Iran's attacks fail.
- Hypothesis H1 (managed de-escalation) + weakly on the Ukraine track (exchange, Europeans at the table); against it, both sides keep striking energy in the same days.
- Hypothesis H5 (loss of control) =: Khor Mor still has no attributed attacker; Iraq's parliament has only opened an inquiry.

## 4. Risk of a wider war
Gulf: two attacks on Bahrain in three days; the next target may be another host country (Tehran has warned that regional states assisting US–Israeli strikes are legitimate targets, ABC News, 15 Nov). Mediterranean: the EU boarded the shadow-fleet tanker MIDAG (IRINI, 13 Nov; Kallas, 15 Nov), widening maritime pressure on Russia beyond the Baltic. Russia: Ukraine's strikes now reach deeper and into petrochemicals (Nizhnekamsk, about 1,200 km).

## 5. Signs of de-escalation
Both Bahrain attacks were intercepted without casualties; Trump leans towards not striking; Ghalibaf publicly keeps the path to a deal open; the war's largest prisoner exchange and European participation in talks are agreed.
