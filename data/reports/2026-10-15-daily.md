## 1. Main finding
Both halves of the "energy ceasefire" fell apart within a day. Ukraine, after three days without refinery strikes, struck back, citing Russia's mass strikes and, in Zelensky's words, the loss of intelligence and Starlink limits. US pressure on one side produced not quiet but an open rift between Kyiv and Washington. The Black Sea crossed a new line: drones now hit ships inside Romania's territorial waters, its president promises a reaction, and NATO is discussing it. The Gulf stays hot: two sinkings near Hormuz in a day, Houthi missiles on Yemen's government in Aden. Markets showed a flight from risk for the first time this week.

## 2. Key new events
- **Ukraine resumes refinery strikes.** Zelensky to the FT: "The block is out. We're done." A senior Ukrainian official confirmed the Ryazan strike to AP. Zelensky told Piers Morgan he felt betrayed by the Trump–Putin diesel deal.
- **Russia's strikes.** 580 drones overnight, 536 shot down, impacts at 31 locations, per Ukraine's Air Force; more than 850 since the start of 14 October, per Zelensky ([United24, 15 Oct](https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/russia-bombards-ukraine-with-over-850-shahed-drones-in-relentless-day-and-night-attack-23473)). Russia's defence ministry says it hit the port of Izmail and two cargo ships carrying military supplies.
- **Black Sea, Romania.** The Comoros-flagged Yara J caught fire on the evening of 14 October and the crew put it out; the San Marino-flagged Wine Trader reported a fire at about 07:00 local time on 15 October, 13 nm south-east of Sulina, with 4–5 rescued and 7–8 missing (figures changed during the day). Both were anchored near the Sulina Canal. Reuters counts at least five other ships hit by drones off Romania and Bulgaria in 10 days, with 23 sailors dead or missing ([Meduza/Reuters, 15 Oct](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/10/15/two-tankers-catch-fire-off-romania-s-coast-amid-a-wave-of-deadly-drone-attacks-on-black-sea-shipping-more-than-half-a-dozen-sailors-are-missing)).
- **Hormuz.** The Cape Dao sank weeks after being hit on 23 September; CNN counts nine attacks in or near the strait since Monday ([CNN, 15 Oct](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/15/middleeast/strait-of-hormuz-ships-sunk-iran-intl-hnk)). US Central Command did not comment on Iran's tanker claim.
- **Yemen.** Yemen's information minister says the prime minister and ministers were unhurt; the Houthis say they targeted a Saudi-run operations headquarters ([Straits Times/Reuters, 15 Oct](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/middle-east/houthis-attack-presidential-palace-complex-in-yemens-aden-pm-unharmed-officials-say)). Yemen's health ministry counts 1,282 killed since 6 August.
- **Sanctions and oil.** The US is weighing relief for some "shadow fleet" vessels (Reuters; Energy Secretary Wright, 15 Oct). The FT reports the administration wants to unfreeze Russian assets and take control of them (one report, via Ukrainska Pravda).

## 3. Links and hypotheses
- **Loss of control** (hypothesis, +): a ceasefire by declaration, with no text and no verification, collapsed on both sides in four days; US leverage on Kyiv (intelligence, Starlink) pushed it to an open break, not to compliance.
- **Overlap of interests** (hypothesis, = to +): Russia got diesel contracts and may now get shadow-fleet relief (the US says it will not help Russia; to be checked). The Kremlin presents Kyiv's strikes as an obstacle to US peace efforts.
- **Deliberate escalation** (hypothesis, +): the Houthis hit symbols of power (Aden, Aramco); Iran calls the ships it says were hit "violators", naming targets in advance.
- **A common design** (hypothesis, unconfirmed): the Romanian fires came on the same night as Russia's strikes on Izmail. That is a sequence, not a proven link; there is no attribution.
- **Markets** (hypothesis): futures fall on stock releases and shadow-fleet talk, while the physical Brent price stays near $126.

## 4. Risk of a wider war
Higher in the Black Sea. A strike inside a NATO member's territorial waters, missing sailors and a drone in Romanian airspace make this the closest point to a direct clash with NATO this autumn. What holds it back: Romania and Bulgaria do not attribute the strikes without proof, and Romania's president answered a question about Article 4 only with "there will be a reaction". In the Gulf, CNN reports US options for a renewed war with Iran. No direct clash between major powers.

## 5. Signs of de-escalation
- The IEA is preparing a coordinated 100 mb stock release, including diesel; Spain is already releasing 4.4 mb.
- NATO channels work: Rutte met Dan and Radev and promised to adjust the posture as needed.
- Iran and the US still exchange messages through mediators (Iran's foreign ministry, 13 Oct), without progress.
- Bulgaria's proposed Black Sea moratorium on strikes against civilian ships will be discussed in the EU (Radev).
