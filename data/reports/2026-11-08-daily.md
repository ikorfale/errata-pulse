## 4. Risk of wider war
The main risk is not Trump's decision, which he has postponed in words, but inertia at sea: two attacks within an hour at Hormuz and the first US use of weapons against a merchant ship in a week. The Saudi–Houthi retaliation cycle sped up: missiles at the capital and the Eastern Province the day after strikes near Sanaa. Khor Mor is the sixth attack since 2024 with no attribution; if strikes repeat, Iraqi Kurdistan becomes a new energy front. Dnipro and Kharkiv show Russia can take a region the size of a European country off the grid in a night.
