## 1. Main finding
The war's map widened, its intensity did not. A US base in Bahrain, the Gulf of Finland and a declared mine zone east of Hormuz all saw fire in the same week as the first trilateral Ukraine talks in nine months and the first oil surplus of the war. The world is adapting to the oil shock but not to the gas shock: Europe enters winter with the lowest storage in over a decade.

## 2. Week in brief (8–14 Nov)
- 10–11 Nov: Abu Dhabi talks; Ukraine proposed an energy and Black Sea truce, Russia declined; Ukraine hit Ust-Luga and the Naro-Fominsk.
- 12 Nov: US envoys met Putin, then Zelensky in Dublin; Trump: no Iran deal needed; unattributed strike on Sirik.
- 13 Nov: IRGC drones at Bahrain shot down; IEA surplus; Volgograd refinery fire, four dead per the governor ([Kyiv Independent, 13 Nov](https://kyivindependent.com/volgograd-oil-refinery-rostov-on-don-production-facility-in-flames-following-ukrainian-drone-attack/)); EU summit named its negotiators.

## 5. Risk of a wider war
Two new vectors: the Baltic and repeated direct Iran–US exchanges. The risk of a clash between major powers is up but not realised: the US answers with interceptions, not strikes.

## 6. Signs of de-escalation
