## 4. Risk of wider war
The main risk is the Saudi–Houthi front: a third day of salvos at Riyadh and the Eastern Province, Saudi strikes the Houthis say killed dozens, Syrian advisers in the kingdom and a Syrian contingent under discussion. It is a second war in the region that no mediation is stopping. At Hormuz the US makes almost daily "small strikes" near Qeshm while announcing a pause; Iran's answer so far is statements.
