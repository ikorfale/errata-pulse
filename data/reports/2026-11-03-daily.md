## 1. Main finding
In one night the energy war reached consumers on both sides: 12+ hours without power in parts of Ukraine, Kyiv's water and transport, rolling blackouts in Crimea, next to petrol limits in Nizhny Novgorod since 1 Nov. For now it is one night, not a new state; the index holds at 68 because the Ukraine track got a date while the Iran track slipped back. The next days decide it: whether 4-queue outages become the norm and whether the US strikes Iranian soil again after the vote.

## 2. Significant new events

- Yaroslavl refinery: fire and damage, two injured (governor via Reuters, 3 Nov). Confirmed; downtime unknown.
- Crimea: power restrictions in Simferopol, Sevastopol, Kerch, Yevpatoria and other towns (Russian-installed authorities, 3 Nov).

- Unrest near Kyiv at a National Guard site holding mobilised men; criminal cases opened (2–3 Nov).

## 3. Links between events and hypotheses
- **Deliberate escalation (hypothesis): stronger.** Both energy campaigns reached consumers the same night, and the Yaroslavl strike right after Kstovo contradicts the informal late-October pause on refinery strikes that the WSJ reported.
- **Using the crisis (hypothesis): mixed.** Many actors aligned to the US vote; election day passed without a campaign against Iran but with acknowledged point strikes. The decision is postponed, not dropped.
- **Loss of control (hypothesis): some evidence for.** US officials describe strikes on Iranian launchers as recurring responses to IRGC fire, not as decisions; that is how exchanges escalate without anyone choosing to. Against: both the US and Iran keep a mediated channel open.

## 4. Risk of wider war
Iran: exchanges of fire around Qeshm have gone on for weeks and are now acknowledged; a downed crewed aircraft could turn "defensive" strikes into a campaign. Yemen: the Houthi push toward Tur al-Bahah threatens the Aden–Taiz road. Ukraine: the Kremlin said it plans no new humanitarian operations (Peskov, 2 Nov).

## 5. Signs of de-escalation
Abu Dhabi on 10–11 Nov confirmed by both sides; Brussels meeting on 4 Nov; Trump and Vance speak of "progress"; Iran's president says the government is authorised to renew a truce; oil down about 9% over two days. None of this has changed the situation on the ground yet.
