For the first time since early September, Iran attacked a US military site in a Gulf state: the Revolutionary Guards (IRGC) said they "destroyed" a US early-warning radar in Bahrain in response to an air attack on a telecommunications tower in Sirik, Hormozgan province; US Central Command said it shot down two Iranian one-way attack drones, with no injuries or damage (Reuters via Jerusalem Post; Al Jazeera; CNN, 13 Nov). ## 1. Main finding
Index 69 (+1): military +1 for Iran's first attack since early September on a US site in a Gulf state, a reply to a strike on Sirik, one day after Trump closed the negotiating track. The drones were downed and the US reports no damage, which is why it is +1 and not more. Oil behaves the other way: the IEA sees a surplus and Brent did not react. The risk has moved from supply to direct confrontation: with no negotiating channel, each such exchange has no exit.

## 2. Significant new events
- Iran–Bahrain, 13 Nov: the IRGC claims a strike on a US radar in Bahrain in response to an attack on a telecom tower in Sirik; CENTCOM: two drones downed, no damage (Reuters via Jerusalem Post; Al Jazeera; CNN). Attack confirmed; result disputed.
- Iran, 12 Nov: Sirik hit by "enemy fire" (IRNA via Al Jazeera). Claimed; no government has claimed the strike.
- US–Israel, 13 Nov: the Mossad director in Washington; Netanyahu presses Trump to resume strikes (Axios via Bloomberg/Rigzone). Sourced reporting.
- Oil, 13 Nov: IEA: fourth-quarter surplus, observed stocks +52 mb in October, Gulf exports 18.4 mb/d (IEA OMR). Confirmed.
- Russia, 13 Nov: strike on the Volgograd refinery, fire; four workers killed (Kyiv Post); a mass drone attack on Moscow with airport restrictions (Ukrainian News, Kyiv Independent). Attack and fire confirmed; downtime not.
- Ukraine, 13 Nov: 12 regions partly without power; strikes on Odesa and Zaporizhzhia with dead and injured (Ukrenergo via Ukrainian News; Euromaidan Press). Confirmed.
- Talks, 13 Nov: Kushner: next round with Europeans, the war's "largest prisoner exchange" in preparation (Moscow Times, Meduza). Confirmed as a statement; no date.

## 3. Links and hypotheses (hypotheses, not findings)
- H2 (deliberate escalation) +: a day after "we don't need a deal", the first direct exchange of strikes on land since September; Israel presses to resume the campaign.
- H3 (exploiting the crisis) =: the US keeps the blockade while the IEA shows the oil market already working around the strait ("dark" crossings with AIS switched off, Reuters via Arab News; a surplus, IEA): the cost of the blockade falls for the West, not for Iran.
- H1 (managed de-escalation) + weakly on the Ukraine track (Europeans in the round, a prisoner exchange); against: both sides keep striking energy.
- H5 (loss of control) =: mines and tanker attacks without a named perpetrator continue (UKMTO: 7 attacks in the week).

## 4. Risk of a wider war
Iran–US: a reply against a base in Bahrain puts Gulf states hosting US forces at risk; Trump's threat of strikes "without hesitation" and the Mossad visit raise the risk of a renewed campaign against Iran. Baltic: Estonia confirmed that Ukrainian drones briefly entered its airspace during the Ust-Luga strike (ERR via Daily Baltic, 13 Nov).

## 5. Signs of de-escalation
The drones were downed without damage and neither side has widened its reply yet; the IEA sees an oil surplus; agreement to widen the Ukraine talks to Europeans and preparation of the largest prisoner exchange; AIS transits through Hormuz at 82 (+11 in a week).
