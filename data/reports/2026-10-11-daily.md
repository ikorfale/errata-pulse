## 1. Main finding
The index holds at 64, but under the flat number the brakes are weakening. A US attempt to stop the energy war by declaration failed the same day: Moscow stayed silent and struck power plants in Kharkiv and Kyiv. Iran stepped out of its exchange with Washington over Hormuz and threatened to widen its target list to data centres and cables. The Yemen front turned deadlier, with the worst attack on a Gulf state in the whole war and an American among the dead, which gives Washington a direct reason to intervene. In Russia, the largest tech company has lost three data centres in four days.

## 2. Significant new events
- **The "energy ceasefire".** Trump on Truth Social (11 Oct): "Effective immediately, there is an ENERGY CEASEFIRE in the War between Russia and Ukraine. Both parties have agreed." Zelensky told Axios it was "news" to him; Ukraine is ready not to strike Russian energy if Russia does the same ([BBC, 11 Oct](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6pvg4jyz1m0o)). The Kremlin had not commented by report time. After the post, Ukrainian officials reported at least two hits on Kharkiv's CHP-5 and a fire, a drone strike on a Kyiv power facility and emergency outages ([ABC News](https://abcnews.com/International/russia-strikes-power-plant-ukraine-after-trump-announces/story?id=137178264); [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russia-struck-kharkivs-power-plant-shortly-after-trump-announced-energy-ceasefire/), 11 Oct). Zelensky says Russia carried out more than 200 strikes on power facilities and 17 on oil and gas sites in a week (a party's claim). It is the second such announcement without Moscow's confirmation (the first was on 14 Sep).
- **What is on the table.** The New York Times (via [Ukrainska Pravda, 10 Oct](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/10/8057404/)): US envoys offered Russia unfrozen assets, a Lukoil-related deal and resumed Belarusian potash exports for a limited energy or maritime ceasefire (anonymous sources, one outlet). Zelensky says Ukraine could hold elections only after a full 60-day ceasefire.
- **Iran–US.** Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran's Supreme National Security Council: the US accepted some of Iran's seven conditions for reopening Hormuz but the reply is "insufficient", and Iran is "no longer in the process of responding" (Reuters, AP, 11 Oct).
- **Hormuz and the Gulf of Oman.** UKMTO, 11 Oct: an outbound tanker struck on the port side by an unknown projectile, crew safe; another tanker reported hit hours earlier. US Central Command, 10 Oct: a US jet disabled the Ocean Molica after it left an Iranian port and ignored warnings (via [Shafaq](https://shafaq.com/en/Middle-East/Unknown-projectile-hits-tanker-in-Strait-of-Hormuz)). Attribution of the tanker strikes is not established.
- **Saudi Arabia and the Houthis.** GACA: the 10 Oct strike on the terminal 3–4 complex killed 12 people of nine nationalities, including a US citizen, and injured 309 ([Gulf News, 11 Oct](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-coalition-vows-decisive-response-to-houthi-airport-attack-that-killed-12-injured-309-1.500706140)). The airport stayed closed on 11 Oct ([Airways, 11 Oct](https://www.airwaysmag.com/new-post/riyadh-airport-closure-extended-missile-attack)), and a projectile hit the same terminal again. The Saudi-led coalition blames the Houthis, calls the strike a war crime and promises a "decisive" response; per AP the Houthis claimed both attacks. Houthi media report Saudi-led strikes on Sanaa airport and areas near Bab el-Mandeb. The Houthis warn airlines away from Saudi airports except Mecca and Medina, while saying they are ready for reciprocal measures to spare airports and ports.
- **Russia: data centres.** Vladimir, 11 Oct: Yandex says drone damage forced a complete shutdown of its data centre; no injuries ([RIA](https://ria.ru/20261011/rabota-2123759292.html); Reuters). The third in four days after Sasovo (8 Oct) and Kaluga (9 Oct). On 10 Oct Yandex added drone and missile attacks to force majeure in its user terms (Kyiv Independent). Ukraine has not officially claimed the Vladimir strike.
- **Ukraine.** Zaporizhzhia, 10 Oct: 23 killed in a strike on an apartment building, per Ukraine's emergency service ([Ukrainska Pravda, 11 Oct](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/11/8057459/)). Kharkiv suburbs, 11 Oct: three killed in a strike on a grocery store (regional governor via Kyiv Independent).
- **Hurricane Isaias.** Gulf of Mexico oil shut-ins peaked at 63–72% (federal data via AP and Reuters, 9 Oct). Landfall near Destin on 10 Oct as a Category 2 storm; the major refineries were spared; about 830,000 customers lost power ([Stephen Innes, 10 Oct](https://thedarksideoftheboom.substack.com/p/hurricane-isaias-batters-gulf-coast)). The US approved an emergency crude exchange from the strategic reserve (WDSU, 11 Oct).

## 3. Links and hypotheses
- **Ceasefire by declaration.** Washington announces, Kyiv agrees conditionally, Moscow stays silent and keeps striking. Russia already has a diesel licence and reported offers on assets without taking on obligations. Coinciding interests (H4): weak plus; the bargaining runs between Washington and Moscow. Loss of control (H5): plus; an announcement the other side does not confirm erodes trust in the channel itself. Deliberate escalation (H2): plus; strikes on energy continue at their previous pace.
- **Hormuz talks stall.** Iran records partial acceptance but steps out of the exchange, three weeks before the US midterms. Hypothesis (H3, unproven): the timing is pressure ahead of 3 Nov. Iran's threat to data centres and cables names the same class of targets that Russia and Ukraine already hit: digital infrastructure is becoming a shared target across wars. That is an observation, not evidence of coordination; the common-design hypothesis (H1) is not supported.
- **Yemen → US.** A US citizen's death gives Washington a direct reason to act; AP notes that US intervention could prompt the Houthis to halt shipping through Bab el-Mandeb, the main Hormuz bypass. This is the shortest visible chain to a new energy shock.
- **Markets (H6):** the hurricane and reserve releases smooth the price (Brent near $104) while Middle East physical losses have not shrunk.
