# Outage Watch — 2026-11-19

- **EU, European Commission internal systems (cyberattack):** HR platform Sysper and other staff systems (payslips, time management) and the JSIS medical-reimbursement system were taken offline for about 10 days; first detected 22.10 per the Commission spokesperson; Sysper back on 19.11, JSIS still down; whether personal data was taken is not yet known; no attribution, no ransom demand. Commission spokesperson Thomas Regnier via Euronews https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/19/eu-commission-hit-by-large-scale-cyberattack-with-data-leakage-unknown (19.11); Politico via Reuters https://www.aol.com/articles/eu-commission-targeted-cyberattack-politico-080245000.html (19.11).
- **Russia, mobile internet in many regions:** DownDetector complaints on 19.11, around 12,000 in Moscow over 24 hours, 5,500 in St Petersburg; mobile data in dozens of regions. Cause not officially named; outlets link it to drone-alert shutdowns (Ufa airport restrictions during a drone warning) and to the aftermath of the Timeweb (17.11) and Beget incidents. Complaint data, not a measured shutdown. https://news.ru/society/pochemu-ne-rabotaet-mobilnyj-internet-segodnya-19-noyabrya-prichiny-sboi-v-rossii (19.11); https://www.mentoday.ru/technics/news/19-11-2026/cifrovoi-shtorm-v-rossii-gde-19-noyabrya-propal-internet-i-kakie-servisy-okazalis-nedostupny/ (19.11).
- **Russia, regional ISPs under DDoS:** Sampo.ru (Karelia) says a cyberattack resumed after an earlier one that lasted about nine days; TIS-Dialog (Kaliningrad) DDoS continues. Provider statements only; minor, regional.
- **USA, NSANet:** a Russian-language Interfax item claims the 17.11 outage hit an NSA centre in Georgia and Middle East operations; no second source, NSA called it scheduled maintenance. Not verified.
- **Banks and payments:** only minor or scheduled items: SBI UPI maintenance window (01:15–02:00 IST), Tink-reported open-banking payment issues at S-Pankki (resolved), Swedbank (30% of users of that flow), Banque Populaire/Caisse d'Epargne. No national payment system down.
- **Local:** Wilmington (Delaware) city government buildings lost internet and phones; cause not given. Minor.
