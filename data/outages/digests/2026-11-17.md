# Outage Watch — 2026-11-17

- **Russia, Timeweb (hosting/cloud, ~700k sites):** network-equipment failure 13:28–14:52 MSK per the company's status page; zones in St Petersburg, Moscow, Almaty, Amsterdam, Frankfurt. The same afternoon DownDetector showed complaints across many regions about Google, Discord, Rostelecom home internet and some banks; no official source links those to Timeweb, and no cyberattack or deliberate restriction was established. Sources: https://istories.media/news/2026/11/17/runet-massovo-sboit-iz-za-nepoladok-u-krupneishego-khosting-provaidera/ (17.11), https://kazan.aif.ru/incidents/details/servisy-nedostupny-sboy-v-rabote-timeweb-17-noyabrya-2026-chto-izvestno (17.11). A viral rumour of a "global internet blackout on 17 November" was debunked by fact-checkers and is not what happened.
- **USA, NSANet (NSA classified internal network):** offline on 17.11 (CBS, CNN sources); the NSA said it happened during "scheduled routine maintenance" and "mission-critical capabilities remain fully operational" (NYT). No evidence of a cyberattack reported. https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nsa-network-down-classified-fort-meade/, https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/17/us/politics/nsa-down-network-outage-nsanet.html (17.11).
- **USA, major banks (16.11):** customers of Chase, Bank of America and U.S. Bank reported online-banking and Zelle problems; Chase said a technical issue slowed some transactions (Independent, Xinhua 17.11). Resolved the same day; minor.
- **Indonesia, DANA QR via Xendit:** payment callbacks delayed, resolved 20:29 UTC 17.11 (Xendit status page). Minor.
- **Official status pages:** one incident of impact major, Anthropic ("Claude unable to join meetings"); the rest minor (Cloudflare Durable Objects and Workers AI, Akamai edge in Singapore/Japan, Twilio voice and SMS, Kraken, Gemini ACH).
