## Main finding

Four days did not change the picture: mobilisation goes on without a decree, through coercion on the ground. Its most visible hotspot now is Omsk, home to a large recruitment centre. Raids on draft-age men in Moscow and St Petersburg are the autumn draft season, not mobilisation. The largest ceasefire since 2022, across the Kherson front, ran to its end and let people be evacuated. But the Kremlin is not ready to widen it, and it expires today. Talks in Abu Dhabi are expected after 4 November. The main scenario is unchanged: a declared wave before the end of the year is unlikely; pressure goes through money and coercion.
