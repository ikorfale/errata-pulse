## Main finding

The picture is unchanged: mobilisation without a declaration, not a declared wave. New this week: Bashkortostan
officially tied its cut in contract payouts to a fall in the number of recruits, and Astrakhan made it legal to defer
payouts when there is no money. Money no longer buys the old flow of men. That leaves two ways out: more coercion on the
ground (already happening) or a decision from the top. There are no signs of the second: no decrees on reservist
training or exit, the Kremlin is busy with the Shenzhen summit, and public interest in mobilisation stays below its norm.
Main scenario: a declared wave before the end of the year is unlikely. The key check is Kluge's October recruitment
figure.
