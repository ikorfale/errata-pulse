## Main finding

The picture is the same: mobilisation without a declaration, not a declared wave. The loudest item of the week, the
claimed Rosstat leak (394,000–450,000 dead, if the database is genuine), speaks to the cost of the war, not to a change in
how the army is filled: the recruitment pace (about 1,000 a day, Kluge) and the payout line are as before. Regions keep
cutting sign-up payouts, and recruitment leans more on intermediaries and coercion. The talks produced a prisoner exchange
and a new round with Europeans, but Russia refused to discuss a ceasefire. Public interest in mobilisation has been below
baseline for four readings, so society −1 under the rule written in advance. Main scenario: a declared wave before the end
of the year is unlikely.
