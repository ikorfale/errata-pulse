## Main finding

The week added confirmation that mobilisation is already under way without a decree, through coercion on the ground. An independent outlet counted at least 33 regions with complaints. A regional budget now pays, in a separate line, those who bring people in. CNN found the same kind of stories, partly through the same rights groups. This is pressure on individuals, not preparation for a declared wave. Direct signs of a declared wave (mass summonses to reservists, exit limits, a decree) have not appeared. The Oleshky ceasefire is the first since this index began to actually stop fire on a section of the front, but it is short. The main scenario is unchanged: mobilisation without a declaration; a declared wave before the end of the year is unlikely.
