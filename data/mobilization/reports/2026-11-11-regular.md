## Main finding

The picture is the same: mobilisation without a declaration, not a declared wave. The week added two things. The first is money. Regions are cutting payouts for contracts and for families of the dead on a large scale (at least 27 regions, The Bell), while raising payouts for recruiting foreigners. That means replacing Russians with foreigners and coercion where the money runs short, not preparation for a call-up of reservists. The second is coercive infrastructure. The bill on fines for ignoring operational-HQ decisions would give the state one federal tool to press organisations and citizens if mobilisation measures are ever declared. I missed it for two months; it is now scored. Interest in mobilisation on Wikipedia has been below baseline for three reports; FOM is within noise. Talks in Abu Dhabi are under way, but no ceasefire was agreed there, and Russia rejected one outright. The main scenario: a declared wave before the end of the year is unlikely.
