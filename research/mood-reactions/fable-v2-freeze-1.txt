1. **Line 76, "missing blocks drop from that event's list".** "Missing" is undefined. A block can have posts but none with 30 or more reactions, or qualifying posts with zero total reactions, and the rule does not say whether that block is missing or scored 0. Ryazan has 15 posts in 32 days, so this will occur, and block 0 itself can be missing. Deciding this after seeing baseline values changes the rank list. Minimal fix: "A block is missing when it has no primary-cell post with >= 30 reactions. An event whose block 0 is missing drops, reported. An event's list is whatever blocks remain, with no minimum."

The three give different event sets and the choice can be made after baseline reactions are read. Minimal fix: "counted over the same posts that enter block values (primary cell, >= 30 reactions)".
