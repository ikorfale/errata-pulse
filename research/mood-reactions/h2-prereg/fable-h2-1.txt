Read all four files. Findings below, blocking only.

## (a) Choices after outcome data, unblinding, v2 leaks

- **Null pool overlaps v2-read data, lines 65-68.** The pseudo hours come from the treated channels' 120 days before the freeze. v2 read day-level alarm and grief shares for blocks -29..0 of every v2 event on these same channels, and most of those windows fall in Jul-Oct 2026. The pool bounds, the 120-day span and the toll >= 1 exclusion were all chosen after that reading. Fix: draw pseudo hours from the study period itself, freeze to 2027-01-31, on the treated channels, with the window clear of events as in (b) below. If a channel has fewer than 10 non-overlapping eligible windows there, extend back to 2026-07-01 but exclude any hour whose window touches a v2-read span, that is v2 hour 0 minus 30 days through plus 24 hours. State the rule now.

- **Norm windows of early events overlap v2-read windows, lines 10-13 and 54.** An event in the first two weeks after the freeze has a norm reaching into October, which may be inside a v2-read span for that channel. The file's own standard is "windows I have not read". Fix: an event enters only if its hours -336..48 do not overlap any v2-read span on that channel. Mechanical, toll and date based.

- **Picking a new treated channel after its event, lines 34-35.** Resolving a username on t.me/s shows the latest posts with their reactions, which within 72 hours of hour 0 is the event block itself. The pick rule has human judgment calls, so this is a choice made while seeing outcome data. Fix: before the freeze, run pick_v2.py and resolve usernames for every region that can become treated under the register rule, and commit that table. A region not in the table does not enter.

## (b) Tests that cannot fail or are biased toward H2

- **Neutral set mixes categories with different base rates, lines 41-42 and 53.** The block value is pooled over weather, sport, culture and crime posts. After a deadly strike, culture and sport posting drops, often by declared mourning, while crime-and-courts posting rises with security content: FSB detentions, "теракт", "диверсант", "наводчик", "корректировщик". None of those words are in STRIKE_V2. Crime posts draw 😡 and 😱 at baseline. So the negative share rises from mix shift alone, in the direction of H2, with no change in reader mood. Fix: drop crime-and-courts from neutral, and add a frozen never-neutral list: теракт, диверс, СБУ, ФСБ, агент, наводч, корректир, трагед, скорб, памят, отмен, перенес. Put the regex file and its sha in the freeze bundle. Without the file, no second analyst can compute D.

- **Classifier check does not test the leak it exists for, line 45.** Precision is measured on control channels in quiet weeks. The failure mode is strike-adjacent text on treated channels in hours 0..48. Fix: a second blind sample of 100 classifier-neutral posts drawn from the treated channels' hours 0..48 of the 11 v2 events, text only. Require that at most 5 are labelled strike-related in any way by consensus. v2 text is already read and these events are not in the H2 set, so this costs nothing.

- **Pseudo windows can contain real event blocks, line 66.** "At least 14 days away" lets a pseudo hour sit 14 days after an event. Its norm then covers that event's hours 0..48. If H2 is true, this inflates the pseudo norm, lowers D*, and shrinks p. Fix: a pseudo hour is eligible only if its hours -336..48 do not overlap any toll >= 1 event's hours 0..48 in that region.

- **Clustered events are treated as independent, lines 61-62 and 65-68.** Combined strike nights hit several regions in one night. Two D_e from the same night share hours and controls, but the null draws their pseudo hours independently, so it is too narrow. Fix: events with hour 0 within 48 hours of each other merge into one event whose D_e is the mean, and K counts the merged event once. Pseudo draws follow the same merging.

## (c) Two analysts compute different D or p

- **Null procedure is underspecified, lines 65-68 and 85.** Not stated: whether the per-event control exclusion and the control floor are applied at the pseudo hour, what happens when a pseudo event fails the treated floor, and what happens when a treated channel has no eligible pseudo hours. Line 85 also says control reactions for Sep-Oct are fetched, while the pool is 120 days, which is July onward. Fix: pseudo events use the full eligibility pipeline at the pseudo hour; a pseudo event failing the floor is redrawn, up to a stated cap, after which the event's draw is skipped and the draw count is reported; a channel with no eligible hours drops its event from the test and is reported. Make the fetch span match the pool.

- **Region universe is open, lines 24-30.** Officials in Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson issue near-daily civilian death statements, which the "Russian region" wording covers. One analyst includes them, another does not, and K and D differ. Fix: list the eligible regions explicitly, for example the v2 register's regions plus others in the Russian Federation's pre-2022 borders including Crimea and Sevastopol, and exclude the four 2022 oblasts for the Belgorod reason.

- **Control region struck, lines 36-38.** A control region with a toll >= 2 event is both treated-eligible and a control. It is not stated whether it becomes treated, with which channel. Fix: regions in usernames-controls.tsv are never treated; their events are logged, excluded as controls per the existing rule, and reported.

- **What K counts, lines 11-12 and 29-30.** Belgorod, Kursk and Bryansk events are "listed and counted". If they count toward K = 12, the stop can fire with four testable events. Fix: K counts only events in a treated-eligible, non-excluded region, and the stop is on logged events of that kind.

- **Placebo eligibility, lines 75-76.** "Still inside no other event's window" has no stated consequence, and the placebo's floor and control set are not specified. Fix: a placebo event whose hours -504..-120 overlap another event's hours 0..48 drops from the placebo, reported; otherwise the placebo runs the full pipeline with hour 0 moved.

**Open questions.**
1. Keep threshold 2. Prospective, so no fork. The shock-size caveat is already stated. Print toll beside each D_e; add no second test.
2. Keep the median DiD with the shuffled null. With K <= 12 a fixed-effects regression adds weighting and clustering choices without adding power. The shuffle is only sound if the pool is fixed as above.
3. The injection check is fair. It uses no real-event data and can fail. Its weakness is that delta = 0.03 and the 0.5 detection bar are unanchored. State 0.03 as the smallest effect you would call a mood change, and have the pipeline print the smallest delta reaching 80 percent detection next to the pass or fail.
