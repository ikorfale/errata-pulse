## New blocking problems

### (a) Choices after outcome data

1. **The toll ≥ 1 register has no source, scope, timing or commit (lines 38, 50, 83, 98).** Four rules depend on "a toll ≥ 1 official death statement" in a region, but only toll ≥ 2 events are logged (line 12) and only control-region events are said to be logged (line 31). Toll ≥ 1 statements in treated-eligible regions that never reach 2, and every toll ≥ 1 statement from 2026-07-01 to the freeze for the extension pool, are not logged anywhere. As written, that register gets compiled after the fetch. Borderline cases are judgment calls: death from injuries days later, a fire caused by debris, a mayor rather than a governor, rescuer versus civilian. Each call moves pseudo eligibility and control exclusion while D is on screen. Two analysts also compile different registers. Fix: a file `h2_tolls.jsonl` holding every statement by the line-35 official list of ≥ 1 civilian killed by a strike, in every treated-eligible and control region, with the statement timestamp as its hour 0 and v2's civilian definition (rescuers count, Kirov-style corrections at statement time). The 2026-07-01 to freeze part is committed with its sha at the freeze. The prospective part is appended within 72 h like the event log and closed at stop + 72 h, before any fetch.

### (b) Tests that cannot fail or are biased toward H2

NONE new. The injection check, the floor, the redraw and the control exclusion are all applied symmetrically to real and pseudo units.

### (c) Two analysts compute different D or p

2. **Pool end, pool bounds and the stop are inconsistent (lines 11, 82-85, 100-101, 108).** The pool is "freeze to 2027-01-31", but the stop fires at K = 12 and the fetch happens "after the stop". If K = 12 arrives in December, one analyst fetches then and draws from a pool that is partly unobserved, another waits until February. It is also unstated whether a pseudo hour may sit within 336 h of the pool start or within 48 h of its end. And the extension trigger, which is my round-1 wording, can never be unmet: a window is 16 days and the study period is 92, so at most 5 non-overlapping windows fit, and the extension to 2026-07-01 always fires. That is deterministic but not what the text implies. Fix: pool_end = min(stop timestamp, 2027-01-31). Pseudo hour 0 is drawn from [pool_start + 336 h, pool_end − 48 h]. Fetch no earlier than pool_end + 48 h + 7 days. The stop is final: no event logged after it enters, and nothing is added after the fetch. Either state that the pool always starts 2026-07-01 or change the trigger to a count of eligible hours.

3. **Merging is ambiguous in two ways (lines 29-32, 40-43, 50, 78).** First, chaining: events at 0 h, 40 h and 80 h are pairwise within 48 h for neighbours only, so one analyst makes one unit and another makes two, and D and K differ. Second, membership: "any regions" on line 40 lets a Belgorod or control-region toll event become a member, but it has no channel and no D_e, and line 42 ("at least one member in a treated-eligible region") implies such members exist. Control exclusion is also stated per unit on line 50 but D_e is per member on line 78, and members have different hour 0s. Fix: members are register events (toll ≥ 2) in regions listed in `h2_regions.tsv`, nothing else. Merge transitively, so a unit is a maximal chain of hour 0s with gaps ≤ 48 h. Control eligibility is per member at the member's hours.

4. **Placebo window rules are underspecified (lines 98-99).** "Another event's hours 0..48" does not say toll ≥ 1 or register event, and the −504..−120 window is not checked against v2-read spans although line 17 says no H2 window may touch one. Two analysts drop different placebo units, and the placebo gates the claim. Fix: a placebo unit drops if its hours −504..−120 overlap any toll ≥ 1 event's hours 0..48 in its region or touch a v2-read span on its channel; control exclusion at the placebo hour uses the same toll ≥ 1 rule.

5. **No fetch-coverage rule (lines 75, 100-101).** v2 needed one after a pager stopped a day short and after the br_gorod hole. H2 fetches seven months on about 60 channels, so truncated pagers are likely. A truncated norm can still pass the floor and gives a different D from a complete one. Fix: carry v2's rule over. A channel's fetch must contain a post older than the earliest window start it serves, else re-fetch before anything is parsed. Any 72-h stretch with zero posts inside a used window is reported as empty.

6. **Norm floor wording (line 72).** "≥ 30 in the norm" can be read as 30 posts or 30 reactions. Write "≥ 30 such posts".
