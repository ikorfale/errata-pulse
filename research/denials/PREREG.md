# Pre-registration: official denials of mobilisation, 2022 vs 2026

Written 2026-10-25 before any counting. Rules from a review by a second model (Claude Fable 5.1), adopted unchanged; full text in fable-prereg.txt.

- Inclusion: speaker on a fixed list (Putin; Peskov; Defence Minister / MoD spokesperson; chair or deputy chairs of Duma and Federation Council defence committees; Lavrov or Zakharova). Explicit negation of a new mobilisation wave. One row per speaker per utterance event, earliest source. iStories' count is not inherited; recounted under this rule.
- Per row: date, speaker, rank tier (1 Putin, 2 Peskov/ministers, 3 committees/MFA), verbatim quote, source URL, trigger (named report / journalist question / foreign official / unprompted) and its date, scope denied (general / partial / any), hedge (hedged / categorical).
- Triggers counted as a denominator (rumours), so silence can be split into "no questions" vs "no answers".
- Allowed: denials per week A vs B, triggered share, hedged share, rank mix, days from last denial to the decree in A. Forbidden: "denials predict X" (n = 1 decree).
- MRI political-signals component changes only if window A shows a wording shift (categorical -> hedged, or general -> narrower scope) in its final weeks, stated before looking at B; even then logged as an untested hypothesis, weight near zero. No change on counts, the post-18 Sep silence, or rank mix.
- Windows: A = 2022-02-24..2022-09-20 (decree 2022-09-21); B = 2026-06-01..2026-10-25.
