## Known gaps / cautions
1. Region-day aggregation (your unit) makes Belgorod special: governor/opershtab post daily totals, and 4-5 deaths/day were not systematically walked for every day (only found via search). Belgorod Jan-Feb 2026 (21 dead in ~5 weeks) and Jul-Oct 2026 likely contain more 4+ region-days. A full pass needs the opershtab31 / Shuvaev Max archive day by day.
2. Night attacks straddle dates (Chekhov 08-03/04, Koloskovo 08-16/17, Dzhankoi 05-05/06): decide a rule (event local time vs. announcement) before freezing.
3. Several "later tolls" are media-only (Kotovsk 8, Arkhipo-Osipovka 8, Belgorod 6, Bryansk 8). Primary Telegram/Max posts were not opened directly; links are to media quoting them.
4. Civilian status is as declared by Russian officials; plant/warehouse workers (Smolensk, Kotovsk, Kirov, Chekhov) are civilians under NGE's method but defence-industry links exist (Kirov AVITEK, Bryansk Kremniy El).
